# Fees

Creator picks a trading fee at launch in the **1% to 3%** band.

That fee is taken on the **quote leg** (BNB, XAUt, or PAXG).

Launch fee: **0.001 BNB** to the protocol recipient (separate from trading fees).

## Split (fixed)

| Share | % | Goes to |
|-------|---|---------|
| Holders | 40% | Paid in the **paired commodity** |
| Buyback | 30% | Buy + burn of protocol token **$RIZ** |
| Protocol | 30% | Treasury / ops |
| Creator special cut | 0% | Creator earns only as a holder |

No creator VIP cut. If you hold, you get the holder share like everyone else.

## Notes

Holder payout cadence is a product target (about every 15 minutes once the distributor path is fully live). Nothing to claim in a custodial sense.
