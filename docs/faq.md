# FAQ

## Is Riz.Fun live on-chain?

Not yet. Contracts are not deployed for public use. The site is a **DEMO UI**.

## What is DEMO?

Launch DEMO saves a coin in your browser (`localStorage`). Cards, mock trades, and the token page (`#token/<id>`) are local. Clear site data and they go away. No mainnet txs.

## What is $RIZ?

`$RIZ` is the **protocol token**. It is the buyback leg in the fee split (30%). It is not the meme you launch. Your launch has its own ticker. See the **$RIZ** page (`#riz`).

## Got commodity rizz. What does that mean?

Brand line. Memes that pair with commodities. Cream mark, black UI.

## Do you hold physical commodities?

No. Quotes are tokenized (or native BNB). No vault. No warehouse receipt.

## Where does liquidity go after the curve?

Locked **Uniswap v4** pool on BSC. Hook fees post-grad.

## Why is the home page not a full commodity list?

Home stays light: live quotes plus the Markets board. Full directory lives under **Commodities**.

## How do holder rewards work?

40% of trading fees go to holders of that market, paid in the paired commodity. Target cadence is every 15 minutes with nothing to claim. See the **Rewards** tab (`#rewards`). DEMO only for now.

## Do you ask for a seed or private key?

No. Connect is injected wallet only. Never type a seed into this site.
