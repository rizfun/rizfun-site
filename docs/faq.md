# FAQ

## Is Riz.Fun live on-chain?

Yes. Connect a wallet on **BSC Mainnet**. Create sends a real launch tx to the Riz.Fun factory (0.001 BNB fee). Launches show on BscScan.

## What is $RIZ?

`$RIZ` is the **protocol token**. It is the buyback leg in the fee split (30%). It is not the meme you launch. Your launch has its own ticker.

Contract (BSC): [`0xf451035b8154d51850aba222df7640815692ffff`](https://bscscan.com/token/0xf451035b8154d51850aba222df7640815692ffff)

## Got commodity rizz. What does that mean?

Brand line. Memes that pair with commodities. Cream mark, black UI.

## Do you hold physical commodities?

No. Quotes are tokenized (or native BNB). No vault. No warehouse receipt.

## Where does liquidity go after the curve?

Locked **Uniswap v4** pool on BSC. Hook fees post-grad. LP stays locked.

## Why is the home page not a full commodity list?

Home stays light: live quotes plus launches. Full directory lives under **Commodities**.

## How do holder rewards work?

40% of trading fees is designed for holders of that market, paid in the paired commodity. Rolling out with the fee path. Creators earn as holders only (no VIP cut).

## Do you ask for a seed or private key?

No. Connect is injected wallet only. Never type a seed into this site.
