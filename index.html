How it works

Pair a meme with a commodity quote. Trade the curve. Graduate into a locked Uniswap v4 pool.

01

Pair with a commodity

Live quotes: BNB, XAUt, PAXG. Your coin trades against that quote from day one. Metals, energy, and ag stay Soon in the directory until mints land.

02

Ride the curve

Bonding curve from about $5k to about $35k. Fixed 1B supply. Buy and sell in the paired quote until graduation.

03

Graduate to locked Uniswap v4

Curve reserves seed a Uniswap v4 full-range pool on BSC. Fees after grad go through the v4 hook. LP stays locked. Pancake is not the default.

FAQ

Common questions.

Is it live on-chain?

No. Contracts are not public yet. This site is DEMO UI.

Do you ask for a seed or private key?

No. Connect is injected wallet only. Never type a seed into this site.

What is DEMO?

Create stores a launch in your browser. Markets, mock trades, and #token/<id> stay local. Clear site data and they go. No mainnet txs.

How do holder rewards work?

40% of trading fees go to holders, paid in the paired commodity. Target: every 15 minutes, no claim. See .

What is $RIZ?

$RIZ is the protocol token and the 30% buyback leg. Your launch keeps its own ticker. See . Contract and explorer stay Soon.

Do you custody gold or oil?

No. On-chain quotes only. No warehouse, no vault claim.

Where does liquidity go after the curve?

Locked Uniswap v4 on BSC. Hook fees after grad. Pancake is not the default.

Why is Home not the full commodity list?

Home stays light: pitch plus the Markets board. Full catalog lives under Commodities.
