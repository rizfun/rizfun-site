How it works

Commodity pair → bonding curve → locked Uniswap v4 pool.

01

Pair with a commodity

Day-1 live quotes: BNB, XAUt, PAXG. Your meme bonds against that quote from day one. The directory lists metals, energy, and agriculture as Soon until mints land.

02

Ride the curve

Constant-product bonding curve from ~$5k to ~$35k. Fixed 1B supply. Trade in the chosen commodity until graduation.

03

Graduate to locked Uniswap v4

At the endpoint, reserves seed a Uniswap v4 full-range pool on BSC. Post-grad fees go through the v4 hook. LP is locked permanently.

FAQ

Straight answers.

LIVE

Is it live?

Yes. Connect a wallet on BSC Mainnet. Create sends a real launch tx to the Riz.Fun factory (0.001 BNB fee). Your launches show on BscScan.

$RIZ

What is $RIZ?

Protocol token. Buyback leg in the fee split (30%). Not the meme you launch. Each launch has its own ticker.

KEYS

Do you ask for a seed?

Never. Injected wallet only (MetaMask, Rabby, Binance Wallet). No seed forms. No key paste.

GOLD

Physical commodities?

No. Quotes are tokenized (or native BNB). Not a vault. Not warehouse receipts.

GRAD

Where does liquidity go?

Locked Uniswap v4 pool on BSC. Post-grad fees through the v4 hook. LP stays locked.

HOLD

Holder share

40% of trading fees is designed for holders of that market, paid in the paired commodity. Rolling out with the fee path. Creators earn as holders, no VIP cut.

Security

Non-custodial. Real mainnet txs.

WALLET

Injected only

EIP-1193 connect. No recovery phrase. No private key fields. You sign in your wallet.

CHAIN

BSC Mainnet

Launch requires chain id 56. Wrong network gets a switch prompt. Fee is 0.001 BNB to the protocol recipient.

SCOPE

What this is not

Not custody. Not a place to type seeds. Not financial advice. Trade size is your call.
